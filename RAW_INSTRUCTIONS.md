# Post-Assessment

Hello valued student,

Welcome to the post-assessment of the Upskilling Together course. In this assessment, you'll be representing [Temporal](https://temporal.io/), a platform for building reliable processes that can wait, resume, and recover from failures. You will be interacting with Lena, a customer who owns and operates Juniper Salon.

## BACKGROUND

Lena runs Juniper Salon, a neighborhood hair salon where last-minute cancellations can leave unexpected gaps in the schedule. When an appointment opens, Lena and her staff currently use a spreadsheet and text messages to contact clients who previously asked for an earlier appointment. Managing those conversations by hand takes time and makes it difficult to keep track of what is happening.

Your job is to understand Lena's goals for improving this process, including how it should work for staff and clients, what can go wrong, and what matters most to her business. You will then create and present a working prototype that addresses those needs and uses Temporal meaningfully.

## Assessment

1. 1

   ### Setup · before the timer

   Create your public repository and verify the starter's neutral demo. Setup does not count toward the assessment hour.

2. 2

   ### Customer Chat · about 15 minutes

   Chat with Lena for at most 50 turns. Learn her current process, users, failures, response behavior, information needs, and business outcome. Do not ask her to make technical decisions.

   #### Open your customer chat

   Paste your assigned token or complete chat link. Reusing it returns you to the same session.

3. 3

   ### Prototype Development · about 35 minutes

   Given your findings from the customer chat, prototype a website with Temporal enabled that meets the customer's needs.

   To help you get started, for this assessment, we've provided some [starter code](https://github.com/john-b-yang/temporal-waitlist-assessment-starter). The starter code provides a working local Temporal server, Worker, API, and neutral browser demo that you will adapt into a prototype based on the customer conversation.

   Clone the starter code and push it to a new public repository; do not use GitHub's Fork button. This prevents submissions from being connected through GitHub's fork network, where students could more easily find one another's work.

4. 4

   ### Presentation · about 10 minutes

   Create 3–5 standalone PDF slides for Lena covering the problem, prototype behavior, simulated or excluded work, and a practical next step.
